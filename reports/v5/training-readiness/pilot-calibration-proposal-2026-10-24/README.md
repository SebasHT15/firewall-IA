# D73-ratified calibration contract

The owner authorizes exact generator/split/preregistration hashes and a full quota, with pilot quota ceil(full/10). Proposed or pending numeric parameters retain their statuses; no final-value ratification is fabricated. The ordinary full build still requires RATIFIED values.

The pilot retains every source/admission/integrity/row/split gate. It writes PROVISIONAL_PILOT, calibration_only=true and training_authorized=false. Shared readers refuse it for EXPERIMENTAL and FINAL training before row reads. The data remain usable for measurements.

ACTIVE_SCHEMA is enabled under D73. Ratifying the definition does not fill this template, authorize categories, pick parameter values, or sign any run. The new schema is separate from D72 REAL_BUILD/AI FINAL authorizations.
